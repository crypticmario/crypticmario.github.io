I am a psychology researcher and data analyst based in Limerick, Ireland. My work sits at the intersection of social psychology, artificial intelligence, and cross-cultural research.

I study how AI systems encode and reproduce social biases — particularly how large language models treat national and cultural identities differently through their safety mechanisms. I combine computational methods (Python, R) with social-psychological theory to make these patterns visible and measurable.

I hold an MSc in Psychology of Global Mobility, Inclusion and Diversity in Society (Global MINDS, Erasmus Mundus) from ISCTE Lisbon and the University of Limerick, and a First Class BSc in Psychology from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka. Alongside my research, I work as a support worker in residential addiction services, where I designed and delivered the organisation's first intercultural competence training programme.
