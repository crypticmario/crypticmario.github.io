MSc Thesis · 2026

Algorithmic Taboos

Dialogical Repression and the Differential Treatment of National Identity in Large Language Models

Built an automated Python pipeline running four open-source LLMs (Llama 3 8B, Llama 3.1, Mistral 7B, Qwen 2.5) across 2,400 experimental trials. Found that safety guardrails systematically produce higher refusal rates for low-passport-power nationalities on negative prompts — safety alignment tracks geopolitical privilege.

Supervisors: Prof. Mike Quayle & Dr. Ana Jovancevic, University of Limerick
